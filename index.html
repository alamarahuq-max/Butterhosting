Host from your own PC: ButterOS

A tiny free operating system that only runs Minecraft servers (Java and Bedrock). Put it on a USB stick, boot any old PC, and open its web page from your phone. It runs from RAM, so the stick isn't worn out, and it backs your worlds up.

Download ButterOS (ISO, about 500 MB) Checksum

1. Flash the file to a USB stick with balenaEtcher or Rufus (DD mode). 2. Boot a PC from it and pick Live USB. 3. Open the address it shows from your phone. Needs 4 GB of RAM or more. A 4 GB machine runs one Java or two to three Bedrock servers.
